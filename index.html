Level0 Studio Tools

A private, single-user tool for managing one YouTube channel.

What it is

Level0 Studio Tools is an internal tool used by one person: the owner of one YouTube channel. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer and works only with the owner's own channel. It is not offered to the public, and it never asks anyone else to sign in.

1つの YouTube チャンネルの持ち主1人だけが、自分の PC で、自分のチャンネルのためだけに使う社内向けの道具です。一般には公開しておらず、他の人にログインを求めることはありません。

What it does

  • Uploads videos the owner made, and schedules their publication (videos.insert, videos.update).
  • Sets thumbnails (thumbnails.set).
  • Updates titles, descriptions and chapters of the owner's videos (videos.update).
  • Adds captions to the owner's videos (captions).
  • Manages the owner's playlists (playlists, playlistItems).
  • Reads the owner's own channel and video status (channels.list, playlistItems.list, videos.list).
  • Reads the owner's own channel analytics, such as audience retention, traffic sources and search terms (YouTube Analytics API).

自作動画のアップロードと予約公開、サムネイルの設定、題名・概要欄・チャプターの更新、字幕の追加、再生リストの管理、自分のチャンネルの動画の状態の読み取り、YouTube Analytics API による自分のチャンネルの分析を行います。

How access works

  • Sign-in uses Google OAuth 2.0, with the channel owner's own Google account only.
  • Requested scopes: youtube.upload, youtube.force-ssl, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly.
  • The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never sent to any other server.
  • There is no server, no public website feature, no advertising and no cookies.

認証は OAuth 2.0 で、持ち主自身の Google アカウントだけを使います。許可の情報は持ち主の PC の中にだけ保存し、外部のサーバーへは送りません。サーバー・広告・Cookie はありません。
